Who we are

Dycom Industries engineers, builds, locates and maintains the telecommunications and digital infrastructure America depends on, through about forty operating companies with their own names, people and places, and one shared standard.

Incorporated
1969

Headquartered in Palm Beach Gardens, Florida. Listed on the New York Stock Exchange as DY.

To connect America.

Our purpose, and the six values every Dycom company shares.

Dycom delivers solutions that build, connect, power, and sustain the digital and critical infrastructure that connects America and its essential industries, for today and tomorrow.

Our scale is local: we combine national reach and resources with the regional knowledge and expertise of our operating companies to partner with our customers and execute complex work across the full project lifecycle.

03

Real work. Done right.

Two programmes run through everything we do: Headway® for safety and Rightway™ for quality.

Safety management system

Headway®

Safety comes before the schedule, on every job and every crew.

  • A job-task safety assessment before work starts
  • Training and mentoring in the field
  • Hazard recognition on every site
  • Stop-work authority for anyone, without reprisal
Our approach to safety
Quality management system

Rightway™

Quality is planned into the work from the first survey to the final record.

  • Planning before the work begins
  • Controls at every stage of the job
  • Training, so standards travel with people
  • Review and continuous improvement
