dycomind.com · Template system

Sixteen templates.
One frame.

The homepage and the Investors overview are built. Every other page on the future-state sitemap is one of fourteen templates, and every one of them inherits the interior frame that the Investors page proved.

We design them in the order they unlock the most of the sitemap, each with its job, its narrative, the components it needs and the content source behind every block.

The interior frame

  1. 1Site header — six groups, Find a job
  2. 2Interior hero — breadcrumb, H1, one-sentence answer, optional listing block
  3. 3In-page index — optional, numbered jumps
  4. 4Numbered sections — the template's own blocks
  5. 5Closing band — one next step, never two
  6. 6Footer — six columns, Motion control

Every template answers

  • Who arrives here, and from where
  • The one job the page must do
  • The story, beat by beat, in Dycom's words
  • Which blocks are required, which optional
  • Where each block's content comes from
  • What Dycom still has to supply
