# Final Design Direction — what changed, and why

**For:** Dycom review team
**Prepared by:** Icreon
**Date:** October 28, 2026

The Final Design Direction is the Consolidated homepage and Investors page, revised against the comments your team left on the Consolidated review. Nothing about the brand system changed — Civic Blue, Signal Green, Fjalla One and Geist, square shapes, real Dycom photography. What changed is how the page is paced, how dense it is, how it behaves on a phone, and a set of specific corrections you asked for.

This document walks the homepage from the top down, then the Investors page, then the changes that apply everywhere. Each item is written as **what you told us → what we did**. The original Consolidated and all of its comments remain available in the review workspace, untouched, so you can compare the two side by side.

---

## In short

1. **Lighter and less boxy.** Cards, borders and filled panels were removed wherever a rule, a photo or plain spacing does the same job. Body text stays at its readable size; the variety now comes from the heading hierarchy, not from smaller type.
2. **A clearer story in the middle of the page.** The three capabilities and the five-stage project journey no longer sit on top of each other looking like two versions of the same list. A "Who we build for" photo band separates them and each has its own job.
3. **The parts you liked best are back.** The number call-out over a crew photo (from *The Signal Works*), the Civic Blue companies chapter, the compact resource layout (from *Every Connection Is Local*), the full-bleed hero film, and the Concept 1 footer with the green signal mark.
4. **Resource Center is in the navigation**, as a sixth group between People & careers and Investors, with a matching footer column.
5. **Mobile works properly.** The capability tabs, the project journey and the footer all become in-place accordions on phones instead of breaking or sprawling.
6. **A real form closes the page**, in place of a single call-to-action button.

## Navigation

**You said:** the mega-menu was preferred over a different layout because it shows nested content in fewer clicks — but you wanted jump navigation added, and the dropdown formatting looked off. Later: the Resource Center was missing from the navigation.

**What we did**

- **Kept the mega-menu.** Every group still opens into its columns on hover or tap; no extra click was added.
- **Jump navigation lives inside the menus.** On the homepage, each group's overview link now carries the group name with a down arrow and jumps straight to that chapter of the page. On any other page the same link leads to the group's overview page. There is no separate row of jump links competing with the main navigation.
- **Resource Center is the sixth group**, placed between People & careers and Investors. Its four columns — Corporate reports, Safety & employees, Suppliers, Candidates — are the same list the homepage Resource Center chapter and the footer use, so the three can never disagree.
- **Dropdown formatting tightened.** Column spacing and type hierarchy inside the menus were adjusted to match the rest of the system. Between roughly 1100 and 1220 px the header pulls its logo, type and gaps in so all six labels stay on one line; below that the phone menu takes over, with Resource Center as its own accordion.
- **Rightway™** replaces RIGHTWAY™ everywhere, including navigation and footer links.

## Hero

**You said:** bring the film back to full bleed, from the top menu down to the blue bar; the corner mark should be green and all the way in the corner.

**What we did:** the film now runs edge to edge from directly under the header to the pathway band, with a Signal Green mark sitting exactly in the corner. The hero is sized by its copy, so on shorter screens the buttons never collide with the band beneath them.

## Who we are

**You said:** you preferred the *Signal Works* number call-out over an image; the row of six values "falls flat" and wanted icons or some other emphasis.

**What we did**

- The company facts are a large number call-out over an authentic crew photo. The call-out sits inside a light chapter rather than becoming a second full-bleed photo under the hero, which had competed with the film.
- The six values each have a distinct, meaning-linked icon — People, Safety, Integrity, Innovation, Customer Focus, Sustainability — instead of a plain row of words.
- The photo scrolls with a gentle depth effect (it drifts slightly slower than the page). It is held still for anyone who prefers reduced motion, and the footer's Motion control turns it off.

## Our companies

**You said:** you liked this section better with the dark blue background — it differentiates it from the two above and breaks up a page that is almost all white and light grey. The directory side felt too large next to the map. The company pop-up copy was repetitive ("go to their website to find out"), and you asked for the operating-company logo in the pop-up. The list and hover behaviour, you said, was "much improved".

**What we did**

- **Back to Civic Blue.** The chapter sits on the deep blue surface again. The map palette was redrawn to belong to that surface: the halftone land texture you preferred is inverted to a lighter blue on the deep blue, office markers are white squares with Signal Green engagement, and the directory is a deeper panel of the same blue rather than a white card. In dense clusters the engaged marker draws above its neighbours.
- **Directory made scannable.** Compact row typography separates the list from the chapter heading and keeps it in proportion with the map. The synchronised hover between list and map, and the accessible map controls, are unchanged.
- **Company card content.** Each card now carries verified office-address context and states plainly what we can and cannot say about a company's services from the data we have, instead of a generic "visit their website" paragraph.
- **Logos:** no operating-company logos exist in the records or assets we were given, so none are shown. They are not fabricated. The slot is ready as soon as the logo set is supplied — see *Open items*.

## What we do, Who we build for, From survey to service

**You said:** you were still confused about the difference between the three-step section and the five-step section below it — they seemed to show the same things. The service links did not need to be in boxes; if they are links they should look like the links above. When switching between tabs 2 and 3 the text jumped.

**What we did**

- **The two sections now have different jobs and no longer touch.** *What we do* describes the three capabilities (what Dycom does). *From survey to service* is its own chapter on white, illustrating a typical project journey in five stages (how a job unfolds). Between them, a new **Who we build for** photo band in Civic Blue names the four customer segments from Dycom's public filings — telecommunications providers, wireless carriers, cable operators, utilities — without naming individual customers.
- **Service links are plain links**, styled like the links in the chapter above, not boxed buttons.
- **Tab content starts at the same height** whichever tab is open; the block no longer grows and shrinks as you move between them.

## How we work

Unchanged in substance: Headway® safety and Rightway™ quality, each with its own button, beside a lineworker photo. The Rightway™ spelling is corrected here as everywhere.

## People & careers

**You said:** this section was better when it was smaller — everything in it can be seen before getting here, so it should read more like a footnote.

**What we did:** the trade listing is compact, with its short explanatory text retained. The careers drawer still opens from here, pre-filtered when you arrive from the closing form's Careers option.

## Investors (homepage chapter)

**You said:** the boxes should be smaller so they sit three across without so much blank blue, and asked for some green.

**What we did:** the three investor links sit across on wide layouts, size themselves to their own content, and sit beside the intro copy rather than stretching to its height — which had left tall tiles with empty blue middles. The intro column is wider so its two buttons sit side by side. The chapter is headed by the green signal mark, and every button carries the Signal Green highlight on hover and focus.

## Resource Center (homepage chapter)

**You said:** you preferred the *Every Connection Is Local* resource layout.

**What we did:** the chapter is a compact editorial list with no bounding box, in four columns — the same four columns that now appear in the navigation and footer.

## Closing: the form

**You said:** can we have a simple form here instead of a call-to-action?

**What we did:** the page closes with a short inquiry form on the dark surface. Fields are transparent with white underlines. The visitor chooses who they are, and each choice asks only for what matters to it:

| I am a… | The form asks for |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Organization, project location, type of work, timing |
| Supplier | Company, what you supply, region served |
| Community member | Location of the work, topic |
| Candidate | Trade and preferred area — these pre-filter the job search |

Submitting shows a thank-you state. In the prototype nothing is actually sent; where submissions should go is an open item below.

## Footer

**You said:** go back to the Concept 1 footer, which was the favourite; it should be the Dycom logo with the green icon; the "mega foot" should condense on mobile.

**What we did:** the footer is the compact Concept 1 layout with the green signal mark and Dycom wordmark, on both the homepage and the Investors page. It carries a sixth column for the Resource Center. On phones its columns collapse into native accordions.

## Investors page

**You said:** it was "so boxy" — did these items really need to be in a box, with so much blank space? You didn't like the bounding box around the resources, and wanted the option of an image preview for the reports.

**What we did**

- **Photography-led, rule-based.** The page leads with a network-rack photo. Results metrics are large Signal Green numbers over a data-centre photograph — the *Signal Works* treatment you preferred — the results package is a rule-separated list, and the call replay is a green-ruled block rather than a filled panel. The resource centre is an open list. No bordered metric, report or resource boxes remain.
- **Report cover previews.** Each featured report carries a cover-preview slot. Until the published cover artwork is supplied, Dycom photography stands in and is labelled as a placeholder on the page; no covers are invented.
- **The same footer** as the homepage, with the green signal mark.

---

## On a phone

**You said:** the lifecycle section looked broken on mobile and should stack vertically; the footer should condense.

**What we did**

- Below 768 px, the three capabilities and the five project stages each open in place under their own number — an accordion rather than a row of tabs with content floating somewhere below. Each open stage ends with a "Next: …" link to the following one.
- The footer columns become accordions.
- The phone menu carries all six navigation groups, including Resource Center.
- Desktop keeps the tabs and the stage track.

## Across the whole page

| You said | What we did |
| --- | --- |
| Everything seems huge and boxy; the text sizing in Concepts 1 and 4 was more varied; not all the containers are necessary. Also: smaller text is not advised for the web, and we need to stay AA for accessibility. | Reduced the number of borders and containers and widened the heading/body hierarchy — while keeping body text at its readable size. Nothing dropped below the accessibility floor; the variety comes from headings, spacing and photography, not from shrinking copy. |
| The signal mark as bullets and functional glyphs is overkill — it no longer feels special, and people expect bullets to look like bullets. | Conventional bullets throughout. The signal mark is reserved for brand moments — the hero corner mark, the mark that opens a chapter, the footer logo. It is never used as a bullet, an arrow or a dropdown indicator. |

| "Change to Rightway." | Rightway™ everywhere. |
| The dark blue background was liked. | Civic Blue returns to the companies chapter and is used for the new Who we build for band, so the page alternates light and dark instead of running light throughout. |

## What stayed the same, on purpose

- **The brand system** — colours, type, square shapes, button style, arrow conventions and the highlight motion — is exactly as reviewed in the Consolidated direction. The Design System in the review workspace now documents the Final Design Direction and renders its real components, so the two cannot drift apart.
- **The map's interaction model** — hover-highlight between directory and map, click for the company card, square markers with the green location pulse, no signal mark on the map.
- **The navigation is the future-state sitemap.** Nothing links out to the current dycomind.com, its investor-relations host or its careers portal; only operating-company websites inside the company card leave the site, and those are marked ↗.
- **The original Consolidated** direction and its comments are preserved as a separate review item so the decisions behind this revision stay traceable.
